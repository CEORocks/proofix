ProofFix operates through nine deterministic stages. Scope binds the namespace, budgets, and safety envelope. Observe collects structured Kubernetes resources, events, readiness, and live telemetry, while redacting Secrets before model context. Hypothesize creates competing causal explanations. Discriminate runs targeted tests instead of anchoring on the first plausible answer. Plan selects the smallest remediation and pairs it with an executable inverse. The Safety Gate rejects out-of-scope targets, forbidden operations, missing rollback, and data-loss risk. Execute applies only the approved change. Verify requires semantic fixture checks and three consecutive service-level windows. Close compiles the Incident Recovery Packet only when every critical claim points to exact evidence. Every observation, decision, and action is stored in an append-only S H A two fifty-six hash chain.
